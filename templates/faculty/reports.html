Mathematical Metric Definitions
Evaluation Metric Formula & Definition Operational Interpretation
Skill Coverage Ratio Mean_{Teams}(|CoveredReqSkills| / |TotalReqSkills|) Fraction of required project skills possessing at least one team member at or above minimum proficiency.
Avg Skill Alignment Mean_{Students}(Sum min(1.0, Prof / MinProf) / |ReqSkills|) Individual technical depth for project requirements.
Availability Compatibility Mean_{Teams}(2 / (N*(N-1)) * Sum Jaccard(Slots_i, Slots_j)) Pairwise Jaccard similarity of weekly schedule slots among team members.
Role Coverage Ratio Mean_{Teams}(|FulfilledReqRoles| / |TotalReqRoles|) Fraction of project required responsibilities covered by qualified members.
Constraint Satisfaction Rate Count(ValidTeams) / TotalTeams Fraction of teams strictly satisfying team size bounds and mandatory skill constraints.
Assignment Completeness AssignedStudents / TotalEligibleStudents Fraction of eligible students successfully allocated to a team.
