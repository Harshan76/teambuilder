Data & Machine Learning Architecture Defense
1. Why K-Means? When historical group project success labels do not exist, unsupervised K-Means discovers genuine latent archetypes from multi-dimensional feature vectors without fabricating subjective target labels.
2. How are Teams Formed? The clustering model exposes profile archetypes; the recommendation procedure consumes these archetypes to enforce cross-cluster diversity and complementary skill set coverage across teams.
3. Future Supervised Extension If a university repository of previous team compositions and project grade outcomes becomes available, supervised models (e.g. Random Forest, GBDT) can be directly plugged in to predict real-world team performance.
