Approach A: Rule-Based Baseline
Deterministic Rules
Calculates fixed weighted match scores & performs balanced allocation. NOT machine learning.
Weights Configuration Total Sum: 100%
1. Technical Skill Match: 40%
2. Interest & Domain Alignment: 20%
3. Relevant Experience: 15%
4. Availability Compatibility: 15%
5. Preferred Role Alignment: 10%
Approach B: AI/ML Recommendation
Scikit-Learn K-Means
Fits unsupervised clustering model on student feature matrix to discover latent archetypes, then maximizes cross-cluster complementary coverage.
Real Machine Learning Architecture
  • Feature Vectorizer: Encodes continuous multi-hot skills (proficiency weighted), domain interests, availability slots, and experience into a 20+ dimension matrix.
  • Scikit-Learn KMeans: Uncovers latent profile archetypes without fabricating artificial outcome labels.
  • Complementary Recommender: Enforces cross-cluster archetype diversity and schedule overlap.
Bounded dynamically by cohort sample size.
Evaluation Integrity: K-Means discovers profile patterns; it does not claim to guarantee team success without real outcome labels.
